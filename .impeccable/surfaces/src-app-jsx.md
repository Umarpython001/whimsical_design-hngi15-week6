# Surface brief — start.ng homepage (rebuild + whimsical refinement)

Scope: the single homepage of this repo (Vite + React SPA), representing the live site's `/`.
Visitor mode: **Persuade** (design is the product; visitor decides and acts: play a game, or learn to publish one).

Audience/job: casual players (few spare minutes, want a Naija browser game instantly); creators selling honest 4-step publishing.
Proof/content: real covers from each game's own address, real creator credits ("by <name>"), live play counts, the Hangout as start.ng's own hub; A–Z of all 188 community games.

Chosen direction (brief-pinned, user-confirmed): inherit the real start.ng visual world — cream paper ground, ink type, single lime accent + lime highlighter `mark` — rebuilt faithfully, refined with a **subtle arcade warmth** layer: tactile card lift/tilt with PLAY reveal, highlighter draw-in, tracked-mono eyebrows, quiet wobbles. Category-default game-store grid styling and glassy/gradient chrome are refused.

Memorable moment: the lime highlighter stroke drawing itself under "Good games." while the featured covers sit one click below — "my next 5-minute fix lives here".

Constraints: D1–D8 in agents_communicate.md (palette tokens verbatim; section order/copy/links preserved; real covers hotlinked; Hangout then top plays; data-theme light/dark persisted pre-paint; reduced-motion honoured; no glassmorphism/glow/floating noise; narrow cards ≥1px line, ≤18px radius).

Unresolved: none material — external-agent QA and fidelity reports may introduce findings handled post-build.
